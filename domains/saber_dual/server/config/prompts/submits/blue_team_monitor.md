Submit your threat detection report. Your submission should include:

1. **Severity and Confidence Level** - How critical is this threat? How confident are you?
2. **Attack Summary** - What attack chain have you detected?
3. **Evidence** - Specific log entries, timestamps, source IPs, and indicators
4. **Affected Resources** - Which Azure resources were targeted or compromised?
5. **Recommended Actions** - What should the SOC team do next?
6. **IOCs** - Indicators of Compromise for threat hunting

This report will be reviewed by human SOC analysts who will take response actions.
